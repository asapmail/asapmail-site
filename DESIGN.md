# Design System

Status: foundation only. Final tokens must be approved after three visual concepts are compared.

## Product principles

- Credible B2B expertise without unearned partner badges or status claims.
- Clear lifecycle systems thinking rather than generic agency decoration.
- One signature interaction: events → segments → journeys → messages → retention.
- Strong editorial hierarchy, restrained motion, and accessible interaction.

## Required tokens before implementation

- Color roles and contrast targets.
- Display, body, and utility typography.
- Spacing and layout grid.
- Radius, border, shadow, and elevation rules.
- Motion duration/easing and reduced-motion alternatives.
- Component states: default, hover, active, focus, disabled, loading, error, success.

## Component policy

- Prefer shadcn/ui plus Radix primitives.
- Every copied component is reviewed and owned by this repository.
- Magic UI or 21st.dev may contribute at most one or two audited components.
- Aceternity UI is a reference unless a specific component passes code and license review.
